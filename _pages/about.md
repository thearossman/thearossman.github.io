I am a second-year PhD student at Stanford University advised by [Zakir Durumeric](https://zakird.com) in the [Empirical Security Research Group](https://esrg.stanford.edu).

I am broadly interested in building practical systems that help operators, researchers, and end-users understand, in order to strengthen, modern networks. I care about the Internet (L3-L7) as it impacts real people. Before starting my PhD, I worked on hypervisor networking at VMWare (Broadcom) and completed my M.S. at Stanford, also advised by Zakir Durumeric. I am grateful to be supported by an NDSEG Fellowship (2026-) and a Stanford School of Engineering Fellowship (2024-25).
